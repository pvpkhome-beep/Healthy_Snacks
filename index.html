Our Story

About Healthy Snacks

“घरची चव, आरोग्याची साथ!”

Healthy Snacks brings traditional homemade food flavours to Amravati. With more than 7 years of experience, we aim to serve customers with traditional taste and quality.

From crispy Chakli, Chivda, and Shankarpali to traditional sun-dried Shevaya, Sargunde, Kurvadya, Papad, and fresh dairy sweets like Basundi and homemade Pedhe — every batch is crafted with authentic Maharashtrian recipes.
